ABC Tech is a mid-sized IT-enabled organization handling approximately 22,000–25,000 IT incidents/tickets annually through mature ITIL-based incident, problem, change and configuration management processes. Despite established processes, customer survey results indicated poor satisfaction with incident management.

- Analyzed approximately 46,000 ITSM records from 2012–2014 to identify opportunities for improving incident management using Machine Learning.
- Performed data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis and feature engineering on historical IT incident data.
- Developed a machine learning approach to predict high-priority tickets, particularly Priority 1 and Priority 2 incidents, enabling proactive intervention.
- Worked on incident volume forecasting across different fields and time periods to support resource and technology planning.
- Developed an automatic ticket classification/tagging approach to predict appropriate ticket priorities and departments.
- Explored machine learning techniques for reducing manual ticket reassignment and improving incident resolution efficiency.
- Analyzed the possibility of predicting Request for Change (RFC) requirements and potential failures or misconfigurations of ITSM assets.
- Evaluated model performance using appropriate classification and forecasting metrics.
- Translated analytical findings into potential business applications for ITSM automation, prioritization, capacity planning and incident management.

**Key Machine Learning Applications**:

Priority Prediction – Predict P1/P2 incidents for proactive action

Incident Forecasting – Forecast future ticket volumes for resource planning

Auto-Tagging – Predict ticket priority and responsible department

RFC Prediction – Identify potential change requirements and asset-related issues
